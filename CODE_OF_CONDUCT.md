# Getyak Community Standards

Getyak projects are built around human judgment, dignity, and inspectable
evidence. We expect the same standards in our community.

## Be rigorous and humane

- Challenge ideas, claims, and implementations—not a person's worth.
- State uncertainty and correct the record when evidence changes.
- Give specific, actionable feedback with enough context to learn from it.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and cultural differences.
- Make room for newcomers without lowering the standard of care.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate
misinformation, abuse of private data, and sustained bad-faith disruption are
not welcome.

Do not upload real personal, candidate, customer, journal, or conversation data
to make a point or reproduce a bug unless every affected person explicitly
authorized that disclosure.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, or restrict
participation when behavior violates these standards or creates a safety,
privacy, or legal risk.

Use GitHub's reporting and blocking tools for immediate safety concerns. For a
project-specific concern, contact the repository maintainers without publishing
sensitive personal information.

Good-faith disagreement is welcome. Dehumanization is not.
